# A2B Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

An end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project analyzing A2B's financial performance, retail outlet distribution, international expansion, menu pricing, and competitive landscape.

---

## Project Overview

This project uses business data from multiple areas of A2B to understand the company's performance and identify meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on:

- Financial performance and growth
- Profitability and margins
- Retail outlet distribution
- Regional concentration
- International expansion
- Product and menu pricing
- Competitive landscape

The findings are presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard designed for quick business decision-making.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into useful insights that can help understand:

- How A2B's revenue and profit are changing
- Whether revenue growth is translating into profit growth
- Where A2B's retail outlets are concentrated
- The extent of A2B's international presence
- A2B's product pricing structure
- A2B's position relative to selected competitors
